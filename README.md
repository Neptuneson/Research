This is a repository of work done by Robert Petro and Connor Phillips on Strongly regular graphs. 
We spent 8 weeks trying to find novel results on strongly regular graphs to see if it could help us prove or disprove the existance of the conway 99 graph.
After 8 weeks we put together our results in a paper that can be read on arxiv:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.17845
